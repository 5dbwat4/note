## Self-Training on Model-Generated Rationales 基于模型生成推理的自训练

So this was all great. And we are very happy that you can just generate rationales from a big LLM and then fine tune a smaller language model on that. But then someone could ask, why not just fine tune the big language model on its own rationales, right? So that's also been explored. And there's a bunch of different methods that do this. I'm going to talk about one of them called reinforced self-training or REST, and it's going to alternate between two stages. The first stage, given a reasoning problem and perhaps the prompt, let's think step by step, I'm going to have the language model generate multiple rationales, OK? And then I'm going to filter these rationales based on whether they give me the correct answer or not, OK?
这一切都很棒。我们很高兴可以从大型语言模型中生成推理过程，然后在此基础上微调较小的语言模型。但有人可能会问，为什么不直接在大型语言模型自己的推理过程上微调它呢？这也已经被探索过了。有很多不同的方法可以做到这一点。我将讨论其中一种叫做强化自训练（reinforced self-training，REST）的方法，它会在两个阶段之间交替进行。第一阶段，给定一个推理问题和提示“让我们一步一步地思考”，我会让语言模型生成多个推理过程。然后，我会根据这些推理过程是否给出正确答案来过滤它们，好吗？

So think about the word algebra problems, someone has three apples, someone else has four apples. You generate a rationale, and the answer comes out to be 7. You keep that rationale, the answer is 12. You sort of leave that rationale out. And then I'm going to do an update step where I'm going to take these rationales that I filtered in my first stage.
