OK. I think people can disagree about a lot of things on architectures, but there is one thing that everyone agrees on. If you take the transformer paper, I think a lot of people say like, the transformer people got most of the things, except this. And the thing that they really did not get right or I think, most people agree, they did not get right is where you put the layer norm. So in the original transformer paper, the layer norm goes in what you would call the residual path. So in the transformer, you have the residual stream that's x, that runs through the whole network. And then you are going to be applying attention, which does some computation. And it will add back a delta back into the residual stream. And then, in order to make sure that these gradients are stable across layers, a layer norm is placed at the end of each of these components. Now, instead of putting the layer norms in the residual stream, there's an alternative, I'll refer to this as prenorm, in which you can put the layer norm outside of the residual stream, but before each of the computations. So you can put it before the multihead attention, you can put it before the FFN. We'll call this prenorm. The nomenclature will get a little bit confusing. You can call this postnorm for now, but let's call this residual norm, because you're putting the norm in the residual layer.

我觉得人们对架构的很多问题可以有不同意见，但有一件事是大家都同意的。如果你看 Transformer 论文，我想很多人会说——Transformer 的作者们大部分东西都做对了，除了这一件事。而他们没有真正做对的事情——我认为大多数人都同意他们没做对——就是层归一化的放置位置。在原始的 Transformer 论文中，层归一化放在所谓的残差路径(residual path)中。在 Transformer 中，有一个残差流(residual stream) x，贯穿整个网络。然后你要应用注意力(attention)，它做一些计算，并将一个增量加回残差流。然后，为了确保这些梯度在各层之间是稳定的，在每个组件的末尾放置了一个层归一化。现在，与其把层归一化放在残差流中，有一个替代方案——我称之为前归一化(prenorm)——你可以把层归一化放在残差流之外，但在每个计算之前。所以你可以把它放在多头注意力之前，也可以把它放在前馈网络(FFN)之前。我们称之为前归一化。术语会有点混乱。你可以暂时把这个叫后归一化(postnorm)，但让我们叫它残差归一化(residual norm)，因为你是把归一化放在残差层中。

---

Basically, all modern language models push the layer norm outside of the residual stream. This is just like a thing that basically, everybody does. There is one funny exception, but it is OPT350M. And if you all are familiar with language models, we know, OPT, in general, was a mess of a language model. And OPT 350M is even more so because I don't why only that model has a postlayer norm in the residual stream. OK. So this is one of the things that everyone agrees on. And so you might wonder, why is this such a unified thing across all of the different models? And if you look at some of the early work, studying, where do you place the layer norm style research, what you really see is that the early motivation for a lot of this was, when you train a transformer, you need to do a warm up. Actually, modern transformer training still does warm up as well. But you definitely need to do warm up when you train. Now, wouldn't it be nice if we could remove the warm up. So that was the initial motivation for a lot of this research. But people quickly realized that removing the warm up had very serious issues in terms of the stability and convergence of these things.

基本上，所有现代语言模型都将层归一化推到残差流之外。这是每个人都会做的事情。有一个有趣的例外，那就是 OPT 350M。如果你们熟悉语言模型，我们知道 OPT 总体是一个比较"混乱"的语言模型。OPT 350M 更是如此——我不知道为什么只有那个模型在残差流中使用了后层归一化。好，这是大家都同意的事情之一。所以你可能想知道，为什么这在所有不同模型中是如此统一？如果你看一些早期关于层归一化放置位置的研究工作，你会发现很多研究的早期动机是：当你训练 Transformer 时，你需要做预热(warm up)——实际上，现代 Transformer 训练仍然需要预热。但你训练时确实需要预热。如果我们能移除预热那不是很好吗？这就是很多研究的初始动机。但人们很快意识到，移除预热在稳定性和收敛性方面有非常严重的问题。

So if you did postnorm plus layer norm, which is basically the original transformer thing, you got this purple dashed line, oh, you just don't converge as well compared to doing something like prenorm, you can ignore the other terms, you would get much nicer convergence even without warm up. So this was the original motivation. But really, what people quickly realized is that moving the layer norms outside the residual stream has some pretty important implications as you make your network deeper and as you start to grapple with stability issues. To me, I think the gradient attenuation issues are the most clear. When you talk to people who do architecture design, I'm not really one of the people that deeply engages in this. But one of the things that people often say is, keep your residual stream clean. So in this case, you have your x's coming in from the on, the prenorm side. And this x propagates all the way up to the top, all the way up to your final output. And that allows gradients to propagate in the backward pass, straight through this. That makes gradient propagation very simple, which improves both stability and signal propagation. And that's what people realized very, very quickly, that if you do something like prenorm in blue at initialization, the gradient sizes remains the same, because you have this nice, straight through propagation in the backward pass.

所以如果你做后归一化加层归一化（基本上就是原始 Transformer 的做法），你会得到这条紫色虚线——哦，就是不如做前归一化之类的方案收敛得好（你可以忽略其他曲线）。即使没有预热，收敛也会好得多。这就是最初的动机。但实际上，人们很快意识到，将层归一化移到残差流之外对于网络加深以及应对稳定性问题有着非常重要的意义。对我来说，我认为梯度衰减(gradient attenuation)问题是最清晰的。当我和做架构设计的人交流时——我不是那种深入参与这个的人——但人们经常说的一件事是：保持你的残差流干净。在这种情况下，你的 x 从前归一化一侧进入，这个 x 一路传播到顶部，一直到你的最终输出。这使得梯度可以在反向传播中直接穿过它。梯度传播变得非常简单，从而提高了稳定性和信号传播。这就是人们很快意识到的——如果你在初始化时做像前归一化这样的事（蓝色曲线），梯度大小保持不变，因为你在反向传播中有这种漂亮、直接的传播。

On the other hand, if you have post LayerNorm, you have these complicated effects that happen, because your LayerNorming, each time, you're going through a transformer block. And that's going to change the norm of your gradients as you go backwards through. So you can see, from the principle of keep your residual stream clean, that prenorm makes a lot of sense. People also realized through experimentation that this also improves stability, in general, that the sizes and frequencies of gradient spikes were improved under prenorm compared to postnorm. And this is a figure from Salazar and Ngyuen, who were one of the first ones, I think, to study this phenomena carefully. I think this is the reason why it's stuck around. Stability and the ability to go deep are both very, very important for modern large language models. And so this idea of moving your LayerNorm outside of the residual stream is one that basically everyone has adopted.

另一方面，如果你使用后层归一化，你会产生复杂的效应——因为每次经过 Transformer 块时你都要做层归一化，这会在反向传播时改变梯度的范数。所以从"保持残差流干净"的原则来看，前归一化很有意义。人们也通过实验认识到，这通常也能提高稳定性——与前归一化相比，后归一化下梯度尖峰的大小和频率都有所改善。这是来自 Salazar 和 Nguyen 的图，我认为他们是最早仔细研究这一现象的人之一。我认为这就是为什么这种做法一直延续下来的原因。稳定性和深层能力对于现代大语言模型都非常非常重要。所以将层归一化移到残差流之外这个想法基本上被所有人采纳了。

So now, if putting LayerNorms in residual streams is bad, why does LayerNorm have to be at the start? Of course, we have prenorm, which is before our computation. But we could have it after computation as well. That's equally good, at least under that logic. And that's exactly right. Many recent models, like Grok, or Gemma 2, or OLMo 2, have this structure where they've moved the LayerNorm after the computation. So it's a postnorm of a kind, but it's outside the residual stream. Other models still actually just put LayerNorms everywhere. They put a LayerNorm here. They put a LayerNorm after. I'll get to this later as we talk about stability. But one of the other lessons that seems to have held up very well is, if you have stability issues, you can sprinkle in LayerNorms everywhere. And that will generally improve stability. It's almost very strange to be saying this, because it's so ridiculous. And yet, that statement has actually been proven right. Every time people have encountered stability issues, they say, oh, but what if we just throw a LayerNorm into attention? Turns out, that works too. We'll get to that later. OK, that's postnorm or double norm, in this case, where you have two LayerNorms here.

那么现在，如果把层归一化放在残差流中不好，为什么层归一化非要放在开头？当然，我们有了前归一化——放在计算之前。但我们也可以把它放在计算之后。至少在那个逻辑下，效果一样好。确实如此。许多最近的模型，比如 Grok、Gemma 2 或 OLMo 2，都有这种结构——它们把层归一化移到了计算之后。所以这算是一种后归一化，但它在残差流之外。其他模型实际上只是到处放层归一化：这里放一个，后面放一个。我稍后在讨论稳定性时会谈到这个。但另一个被证实的经验是：如果你有稳定性问题，你可以在各处"撒上"层归一化，这通常会改善稳定性。说这话感觉很奇怪，因为听起来很荒谬，但事实证明确实如此。每次人们遇到稳定性问题，他们就会说——哦，但如果我们在注意力中扔一个层归一化呢？结果证明那也有用。我们后面会讲到。好，这就是后归一化或双归一化(double norm)的情况——这里你有两个层归一化。

So given this, you might ask, is there a nice way that we can truly have this relative embedding? And the idea is very cool. It's really just looking at other properties about angles and cosines. So we want our embeddings to be invariant to absolute positions. And we know that inner products of any kind are invariant to arbitrary rotation. So the idea is to say, I'm going to take my semantic word vectors, the ones that are independent of any position. So this is my starting point. And then I'm going to rotate each of these vectors, in this case, in 2D, based on the position that the words appear. So just as a simple example, let's say we have the sentence, "We know that." "We" appears as at position 0, so I'm not going to touch that at all. I'm just going to keep that where it is. The word "no" is at position 1, so I'm going to rotate it by some angle. And that's my one position rotation. Now, what happens if I apply the same idea to the following sequence? Of course, we know. In this case, "we" and "no" are still adjacent. They're right next to each other, but their absolute position has shifted. "Of course" comes before "we know now." In this case, I'm going to rotate the word "we" by two positions, because it's two index, 0, 1, 2. So the word "we" is in the second position number 2, so I rotate by two. I rotate "no" by three positions, because it's in position number 3. And what do you know the relative angle between these two is still separated by 1. So this is a very, very simple idea of just using rotations to represent position. And if we do that, then anytime we take an inner product, those inner products are going to be invariant of absolute positions.

鉴于此，你可能会问：有没有一种好方法让我们真正拥有这种相对嵌入？这个想法非常酷。它实际上只是利用了关于角度和余弦的其他性质。我们希望嵌入对绝对位置不变。我们知道任意类型的内积对任意旋转都是不变的。所以想法是：取我的语义词向量——那些与任何位置无关的向量——这是我的起点。然后根据词出现的位置，旋转每个向量——这里是在二维中旋转。举个简单的例子，假设我们有句子 "We know that"。"We" 出现在位置 0，所以我完全不动它，保持原样。"no" 在位置 1，所以我将它旋转某个角度。这就是一个位置的旋转。如果我对接下来的序列应用相同的想法呢？"Of course we know"——在这个例子中，"we"和"no"仍然相邻，但它们的绝对位置发生了偏移。"Of course"出现在"we know"之前。这种情况下，我将"we"旋转两个位置（因为索引是 0,1,2，它在位置 2），将"no"旋转三个位置（因为它在位置 3）。这两个之间的相对角度仍然相隔 1。所以这是一个非常简单的用旋转表示位置的想法。如果我们这样做，那么任何时候我们做内积，这些内积将对绝对位置不变。

Some of them rotate very quickly, so they capture things like, are they neighbors to each other? And then at the end, after I've rotated every pair of vectors, I get my final embedding.
