现在你可能会说：好的，策略梯度很棒，我喜欢策略梯度。但策略梯度的问题在于，我每次想做一个优化步骤时都需要采样。而采样非常昂贵。我们在系统部分已经讨论过，推理（inference）通常非常复杂和困难，而训练（training）在算术上非常密集且好。所以我想一次展开（roll out），然后想多次重用这次展开。这就是所谓的离策略（off-policy）。要做离策略，我想走多步，但不能走得太远，因为如果走得太远，我对局部奖励的估计就会发散。这就是所谓的 TRPO（Trust Region Policy Optimization，信任区域策略优化）。基本思想是：我想取策略梯度，但我想在停留在我当前所在位置附近的同时，做一些称为重要性权重修正（importance weighting correction）的东西。我不会深入细节。最后，PPO 说：TRPO 是个好主意，但这个距离约束实际上有点难以处理。所以我将提出一个启发式的裁剪（clipping）机制，通过它来阻止 RL 算法走到离原始策略太远的地方。我将在下一讲中详细讲解这些。所以你现在不需要担心。但主要进展是：从策略梯度到离策略，你最后得到了 PPO。希望你们大概理解了。

So many people have tried, many different iterations. And in the end, we do now have a thing that is much less complex than PPO, works pretty well. And is, in many ways, looks just like SFT. PPO 的等式看起来已经有点棘手了。所以很多年来，很多人问了下面这个问题：我们能不能摆脱 PPO？这对很多人来说是一件大事。很多理性的人想出了很多摆脱 PPO 的好方法。我会把它们告诉你们，这样你们就不一定在自己的研究中重复它们了。例如，你可以说：我有一对示例，我可以做 SFT，但用一种特殊的 SFT。对于好的那一对，我会在前面加一个"好"标记（good token）；对于坏的那个，我会在前面加一个"坏"标记（bad token）。所以你会附加一些标记。然后当我生成时，我只在回答前加上"好"，这样我的模型就只生成好的东西。所以这是把 PPO 简化为 SFT，或者说把 RL 简化为 SFT。它不 work。人们尝试过只在好的东西上训练模型——也不太好。你可以训练奖励模型，获得 LLM 输出，只选择奖励模型选中的东西并在其上训练——这也不太好，虽然它一定程度上有效。所以很多人尝试了很多不同的迭代。最后，我们确实有了一种比 PPO 简单得多、效果相当不错的东西，而且在很多方面看起来就像 SFT——这就是 DPO（Direct Preference Optimization，直接偏好优化）。

And so the algorithm is very, very simple. What we're going to do is we're going to try to get rid of the complicated parts of PPO. What are we going to get rid of? We're going to get rid of the reward model. We're going to get rid of anything that has to do with on-policy stuff. And by getting rid of these two things, we're going to try to make a much more simple RLHF algorithm. And it relies on a very simple intuition. Everything in deep learning is just taking gradient steps in the direction of good things. And so we're going to take steps in the direction of the log loss of the good stuff. And then we will take negative gradient steps on the direction of the bad stuff. In other words, we're going to reverse for the log probability of anything that is bad. This is basically the most naive thing you can do. Because you're doing SFT on one hand, and you're doing kind of negative SFT on the bad thing. It turns out that if you wait these two appropriately, you will get an algorithm that is actually pretty good.

所以这个算法非常非常简单。我们要做的是试图摆脱 PPO 中复杂的部分。我们要摆脱什么？我们要摆脱奖励模型。我们要摆脱任何与在策略（on-policy）有关的东西。通过摆脱这两样东西，我们要尝试做一个更简单的 RLHF 算法。它依赖于一个非常简单的直觉：深度学习中的所有事情都是沿着好的方向取梯度步长。所以我们要沿着好东西的对数损失（log loss）方向取步长，然后沿着坏东西的方向取负梯度步长。换句话说，我们要反转任何坏东西的对数概率。这基本上是你所能做的最天真的做法——因为你一方面在做 SFT，另一方面在做坏的方面的"负 SFT"。事实证明，如果你适当地加权这两者，你会得到一个实际上相当不错的算法。

And I'll give you the derivation because it's all very simple. So remember, our goal is to optimize this equation over here. So this should look familiar to you. The first term is my expected reward under my policy pi theta. The second term is saying that's my KL distance. I want my policy to remain close to my reference. That's what I pretrain. Now, I'm going to make a assumption. This is the one strong assumption that I'm going to make. And I'm going to assume that pi, my policy, is actually not a neural network at all. It is the set of all possible policies. It is a nonparametric thing. It can approximate anything. So if pi can be anything, I can actually solve that top problem closed form. I actually know what the minimizer is. And the minimizer is the following. The minimizer is actually to take my original reference policy, and to exponentially tilt it by my reward. In other words, every response gets upweighted or downweighted with a weight, x of 1 over beta r based on how good the reward is. This makes a lot of sense. If the reward is really bad, I'm going to exponentially downweight. If the reward is really good, I'm going to exponentially upweight it. So this is the closed form solution if pi can be anything.

我来给出推导过程，因为都很简单。记住，我们的目标是优化这个等式。这应该看起来很熟悉了。第一项是我在策略 pi_theta 下的期望奖励。第二项说的是我的 KL 距离——我希望我的策略保持接近我的参考策略（reference policy），这就是我预训练的那个。现在，我要做一个假设。这是我要做的一个强假设。我假设 pi——我的策略——实际上根本不是一个神经网络。它是所有可能策略的集合，是一个非参数（nonparametric）的东西，它可以逼近任何东西。所以如果 pi 可以是任何东西，我实际上可以解析地（closed form）解决上面那个问题。我实际上知道最小值是什么。最小值是这样的：它实际上是取我原始的参考策略，并用我的奖励对其进行指数倾斜（exponentially tilt）。换句话说，每个回答根据奖励的好坏，以 1/beta * r 的权重被向上或向下加权。这很有道理——如果奖励非常差，我会指数级地降低权重；如果奖励非常好，我会指数级地增加权重。所以这就是如果 pi 可以是任何东西时的闭式解。

Now, we can then-- now that this pi of r, this perfect solution, I can solve for the implied reward. In other words, I can solve for what is the r that would induce this. And so I can solve for that. I can put the pi on the left side, and I can solve for the reward that we would have. And then we can take that implied reward that I wrote down. And then I can solve for what would happen in the original RLHF objective. I plug-in my reward into that objective here, into my loss. And this gives the DPO objective, which is very intuitive. It basically says take gradient steps in the positive direction, and then take negative gradient steps in the bad direction. You can already see what's going to happen. 现在我们可以——既然有了这个 pi*(r)——这个完美解——我可以解出隐含的奖励（implied reward）。换句话说，我可以解出什么样的 r 会诱导出这个 pi*。然后我可以解出它。我可以把 pi* 放在左边，解出我们将会有的奖励。然后我可以取我写下的那个隐含奖励，然后代入到原始的 RLHF 目标中，代入到我的损失函数中。这就得到了 DPO 目标，它非常直观：它基本上说沿着正方向取梯度步长，然后沿着坏的方向取负梯度步长。你已经可以看到会发生什么——这里的第一个项是好东西，右边的项是坏东西，它们会互相平衡。

OK. And so if I take the gradient of this, I think this is the most intuitive form of DPO, at least to me, you have two things. For every pair that you've had annotated, I'm going to increase the likelihood of the example, that one, and I'm going to decrease the likelihood of the example that lost. And I'm going to scale the step size in some sense by how much my implied reward model is wrong. Did my model already assign a very high reward to the winning one? If it did so, I will take a small step. But if I was very wrong, and I said, oh, these two are almost equal, I had same probability. But in that case, I will actually take a much bigger step size. 好的，所以如果我取它的梯度——我认为这是 DPO 最直观的形式，至少对我来说——你有两件事。对于每一对你已经标注过的例子，我要增加那个胜出示例的可能性，同时降低失败示例的可能性。而且我要在某种意义上根据我的隐含奖励模型有多错来缩放步长：我的模型是否已经为胜出者分配了非常高的奖励？如果是，我会走一小步。但如果我错得很厉害，我原本认为这两个几乎相等——概率相同——但在那种情况下，我实际上会走大得多的一步。所以基于概率差异，我会在这个微分对数概率目标（differential log prob objective）上走更大或更小的步长。所以希望这个梯度目标的作用对大家来说是清晰的。
