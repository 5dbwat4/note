For \textbf{stop-and-wait}, utilization is the fraction of each send--ACK cycle spent transmitting a data frame:
\[
U=\frac{T_{\text{frame}}}{T_{\text{frame}}+2T_{\text{prop}}+T_{\text{ACK}}},
\qquad T_{\text{frame}}=\frac{\text{Frame size}}{\text{Data rate}}.
\]

Assume no errors or processing delays, and negligible ACK transmission time unless specified.

\textbf{1. Short link, negligible ACK size}

\[
T_{\text{frame}}=\frac{4000}{10\times10^6}
=400\,\mu s
\]

\[
U=\frac{400}{400+2(5)}
=\boxed{97.56\%}
\]

\textbf{2. Short link, 100-bit ACK}

Assuming the ACK uses the same 10 Mbps rate:

\[
T_{\text{ACK}}=\frac{100}{10\times10^6}=10\,\mu s
\]

\[
U=\frac{400}{400+2(5)+10}
=\boxed{95.24\%}
\]

\textbf{3. Satellite link, negligible ACK size}

The given \(270\text{ ms}\) is the \textbf{one-way} propagation delay.

\[
T_{\text{frame}}=\frac{4000}{56\times10^3}
\approx71.43\text{ ms}
\]

\[
U=\frac{71.43}{71.43+2(270)}
=\boxed{11.68\%}
\]

The satellite link has low utilization because the sender spends most of each cycle waiting for the acknowledgment.
